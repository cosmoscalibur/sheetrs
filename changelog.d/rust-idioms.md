### Changed

- **Breaking:** Rust idioms across the libraries. `"T!A3".parse::<Reference>()` replaces `Reference::parse`, `Scope::from(text)` replaces `sheetrs_edit::parse_scope`, `"1904".parse::<DateSystem>()` replaces the engine's `parse_date_system`, `Workbook::sheet` and `Sheet::cell` replace `get_sheet` and `get_cell`, and `loader::from_workbook(wb, Format)` takes the format and not a `bool`. `#[non_exhaustive]` on `Level`, `LogFormat`, `DateValue`, the linter's `OutputFormat`, `RuleCategory`, `LinkStatus` and `InterruptionKind`. The modules nobody uses outside their crate are private (`sheetrs_engine::{casemap, collate, locale_data, notes}`, `sheetrs_compiler::{cellstore, formula}`, `sheetrs_linter::{human, json}`, `sheetrs_formula::date_system`, `sheetrs_query::matcher`).
- Every publishable crate has a README, a description that says what it is, and the workspace keywords and categories; the public types are `Send + Sync` (a test per crate); the entry points have documented examples.
