### Added

- `sheetc` implements more functions of LibreOffice with oracle rows computed by LibreOffice 26.8: `WEEKS`, `MONTHS`, `YEARS`, `ISLEAPYEAR`, `DAYSINMONTH`, `DAYSINYEAR`, `WEEKSINYEAR`, `ROUNDSIG`, `SERIESSUM`, `RAWSUBTRACT`, `AREAS`, `SHEET`, `SHEETS`, `HYPERLINK` and `ERRORTYPE` (see `FIDELITY.md`, *Calendar periods, series and sheet information*).
- `ERRORTYPE` of `#VALUE!` stops the build: LibreOffice has several internal codes behind it.
