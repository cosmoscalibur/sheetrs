### Changed

- The names of the actions follow the words of the LibreOffice Calc interface, the same in the CLI, the Rust crates and the Python binding: `delete` for sheets, names, rows and columns (`sheetrs edit --delete-sheets`, `--delete-names`, `--delete-rows`, `--delete-columns`; `Editor::delete_sheets`, `delete_names`; before, `--remove-sheets`, `--remove-ranges`, `--delete-cols`), `formula_to_value` for `convert_to_values` (`--formula-to-value`), `--list-names` for `--list-ranges`, `Editor::write(path)`, `Editor::write_in_place()` and `Editor::write_to(out)` (the idiomatic Rust `write` family; the Python binding keeps `save(path=None)`) for `save(path)` and `overwrite()`.
- `sheetc run` is `sheetc calculate` (and `Program::execute` and `Engine::run` are `calculate`, the module `run` of the engine and of the protocol is `calculate`), `sheetc calc` is `sheetc evaluate`, and `sheetc verify --recalc` is `--recalculate`. The plan that `explain` prints says `delete sheets`, `delete names` and `formula to value in`.
