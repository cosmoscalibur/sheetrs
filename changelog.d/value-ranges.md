### Changed

- **Breaking:** the answer of a calculation is always `{"valueRanges": [{"range", "name", "values"}]}`, as the batch read of a spreadsheet API answers, and the request is `inputs`, `ranges` and `majorDimension`; nothing calls the answer "outputs" any more (`outputs` is only the declaration of what a compiled program exposes: the `settings` sheet, `--settings-file`, `sheetc.toml`). A compiled program or `sheetc calculate` with outputs declared and no `ranges` no longer answers `{"outputs": {name: value}}`: it answers one `valueRanges` entry per declared output, with its `name` and its `range`, and the values of a single cell are `[[value]]` like those of any range; `majorDimension` applies. In Rust `sheetrs_protocol::Outputs` (an enum of two shapes) is the struct `ValueRanges`, `BookEdit::outputs` is `BookEdit::value_ranges` and `Report::outputs` is `Report::value_ranges`. In Python the class `Outputs` is `ValueRanges` and `Book.outputs()` and `Report.outputs` are `Book.value_ranges()` and `Report.value_ranges`. In WebAssembly the class `Outputs`, `Book.outputs()` and `Report.outputs` are `ValueRanges`, `Book.valueRanges()` and `Report.valueRanges`.
