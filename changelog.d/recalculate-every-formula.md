### Fixed

- `--recalculate` and `--embed-template` take the position of every formula from the compiled program (`Program::formula_cells`), not from a second read of the workbook: every formula of the workbook reaches the program and is written back (array cells, shared formulas and formulas that fold to constants included), in XLSX and ODS.
- `TEXTSPLIT` of an empty text, and `ROWS`/`COLUMNS` of a text, give `Err:504` as LibreOffice does; `FILTER` over a blank include column keeps nothing (the `if_empty` value) instead of `Err:533`. Two oracle gaps close.
- Errors per input as LibreOffice 26.8 gives them: `TEXTJOIN` takes a range or an array as delimiter and `TEXTBEFORE`/`TEXTAFTER` give an error of the value for "not found" even when the text is found; `ROWS`, `COLUMNS`, `SHEET`, `XMATCH` and `FILTER` of what is no range, and `FILTER` with a text or a table as include, `INDEX` with a negative index or area, `FACT(171)`, `IMPOWER` with a text power, `IMSUM`/`IMPRODUCT`/`SERIESSUM` over a range with an error, `WRAPROWS`/`WRAPCOLS` with size 0 or negative, `REPT` with a negative count or a text of 2^28 characters or more, the inverse chi-squared at 3,401,607 degrees of freedom or more (`Err:523`) and `LINEST`/`LOGEST`/`TREND`/`GROWTH` with fewer observations than coefficients; the database functions take a block of names alone as "every record" and refuse a blank name in it; a call with the wrong number of arguments gives `Err:504`/`Err:511`/`Err:508` instead of a plain `#VALUE!`. `docs`: `sheetrs-compiler/docs/FIDELITY.md` lists what is left.
