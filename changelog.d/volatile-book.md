### Added

- `now` and `seed` are properties of the workbook, like its locale, and every tool that builds or calculates takes them from it (issue #106): `Book::with_volatile` in Rust (the documented way to initialise them, with `Volatile::or` and `Volatile::configured`), `Book(..., now=, seed=)` and `evaluate(formula, now=, seed=)` in Python, `new Book(bytes, locale, {now, seed})` in WebAssembly, with `book.now` and `book.seed` to read them. The `now` and `seed` of a single call (`calculate`, the options of `inspect`, `compile` and `evaluateFormula`) win over the book's, field by field.
- `now` and `seed` in `[global]` of `sheetrs.toml` are the defaults of `--now` and `--seed` of `sheetrs edit`. The tools of `sheetc` (`build`, `calculate`, `evaluate`, `verify` and the executables) already took `--now`, `--seed` and the keys of `sheetc.toml`; the table of tools and flags is in the README of `sheetrs-compiler`, and the executables, `calculate`, `evaluate` and `verify` have tests on XLSX and ODS now.
