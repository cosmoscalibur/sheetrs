### Fixed

- A range given to a function of a formula that is not an array formula is met by the cell of the formula as in LibreOffice 26.8, in any sheet (the engine only did it within the sheet of the formula): a range of one column by its row, one of one row by its column, any other by both, and `#VALUE!` where they do not meet (`Data!A1:A8*2`, `IFERROR(Data!A1:A3, 0)`). Only the arguments LibreOffice takes as arrays see the whole range (`SUMPRODUCT`, `MMULT`, `CORREL`, `SLOPE`, `FORECAST`, `MODE`, the condition of `FILTER`, the arrays of `XLOOKUP` and `XMATCH`…); the rest meet the cell, including `INDEX`, `SORT`, `SORTBY`, `UNIQUE`, `TAKE`, `DROP`, `TOCOL`, `HSTACK`, `TREND` and `GROWTH` (`INDEX(A1:A8*2, 1)` is `#REF!`, `SORT(A1:A8*2)` is `Err:504`). A range of several cells in the second argument of `DEC2BIN` and the other base conversions, `DELTA`, `GESTEP` and `ERF`, and in the basis of `YEARFRAC`, is `Err:502`. `scripts/probe_intersection.py` asks LibreOffice for every function of the registry and the engine for the same calls.
- An array written in a formula may hold any expression and rows of any width where a spreadsheet takes constants: what is not a constant, or has rows of different widths, is `Err:539` for the whole formula (`{1;A1}`, `{1,2+3}`, `{1,2;3}`), as in LibreOffice, instead of stopping the build. `sheetrs-formula` parses them.
- The database functions take an array written in the formula as the table or as the criteria (`DSUM({"Name","Age";"Ann",30}, "Age", {"Age";">26"})`) as LibreOffice does: in an array a number, a boolean or an empty text is no condition, no record is taken from a table written in the formula when there is no condition, and its text criteria match the whole text of the cell with no wildcards.
- `XLOOKUP` of a block, or with a result array of a different length, is `Err:504` as in LibreOffice, instead of a panic of the engine; `SUMPRODUCT` counts a boolean as 1.
