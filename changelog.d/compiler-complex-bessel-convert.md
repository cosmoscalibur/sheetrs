### Added

- `sheetc` implements the complex-number functions (`COMPLEX`, `IMREAL`, `IMAGINARY`, `IMABS`, `IMARGUMENT`, `IMCONJUGATE`, `IMSUM`, `IMSUB`, `IMPRODUCT`, `IMDIV`, `IMPOWER`, `IMSQRT`, `IMEXP`, `IMLN`, `IMLOG10`, `IMLOG2`, and the trigonometric and hyperbolic functions with their reciprocals), `BESSELI`, `BESSELJ`, `BESSELK`, `BESSELY` and `CONVERT` with oracle rows computed by LibreOffice 26.8 (see `FIDELITY.md`, *Complex numbers, Bessel functions and `CONVERT`*). The results follow LibreOffice's algorithms and the C library's formulas to the last digit.
