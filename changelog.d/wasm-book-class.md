### Changed

- **Breaking:** `sheetrs-wasm`: the package has one object, `Book` (made from bytes; format from the content; statistics at open as shortcut properties `format`, `sheetNames`, `sheets`, `definedNames`, `dateSystem`, `hasMacros`, `total*`), with `lint`, `query`, `range`, `find`, `inspect`, `compile`, the operations of the plan (`deleteSheets`, `deleteNames`, `formulaToValue`, `deleteRows`, `deleteColumns`, `replace`, `calculate`; not a sequence: the planner orders them), `save`, `outputs`, `explain`, `report`, `dates` and `onDiagnostic`. Results are typed objects (`LintReport`, `Stats`, `Inspection`, `Outputs`, `Report`) with explicit `toJSON()` / `toSarif()`. The functions `lint_workbook*`, `workbook_stats`, `get_workbook_stats`, `query_workbook`, `edit_workbook`, `explain_edit`, `calculate_workbook`, `inspect_workbook` and `compile_workbook` are gone, and the free ones are `lintRules`, `evaluateFormula` and `initPanicHook`. Breaking; the API is experimental.
